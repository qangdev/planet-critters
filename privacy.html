Planet Critters

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Planet Critters is made by kats Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and who receives it.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, scores and settings are saved on your device only. We do not run our own servers, accounts or analytics, and we never receive this data. Deleting the game deletes it.

Game Center leaderboards (optional)

If you choose Connect on a leaderboard, the game sends your best Story and Endless scores, the level you reached, and a country code (from your saved choice or your device's region) to Apple Game Center. Other players can see them next to your Game Center name. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can choose Pause sharing at any time to stop future uploads. Scores already on Game Center are managed by Apple.

Ads

The game shows an ad only when you choose "Watch ad to rewind" after losing a level. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, crash and performance data, and device identifiers. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Language & display → Privacy choices. You can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Planet Critters is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Selling data

We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
